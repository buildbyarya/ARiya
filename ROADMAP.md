# Satella Roadmap

## Shared
- Shared calendar: regular monthly calendar with shared events.
- Home integration will be added after the room is polished.

## Games
- Drawing Swap: two-player 45-second drawing exchange, 3m45s session, final reveal.
- More two-player games: Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four and other small games are planned.

## Chat
- Rich Notes/MS Word-style editor.
- Replies that jump to the original message.
- Pinned messages.
- Temporary images: 3 views or 24 hours.
- Temporary voice messages: 3 plays or 24 hours.
- Shared chat background and appearance settings.
